Application readiness, national (fixed)

Share of tests meeting each application's bandwidth and latency requirement, test-weighted. Voice 64 Kbps / 200 ms · HD 5 Mbps · UHD 25 Mbps · Cloud gaming 44 Mbps / 25 ms.

Illustrative only, not a result of the paper. A constant-growth trend fitted to 12 quarters and extended 5 years. The shaded band is an 80% prediction interval; real networks change in steps (new cables, policy, operators) that a trend cannot see.
Data: Ookla® Speedtest® Open Data (fixed and mobile, 2023-Q1 to 2025-Q4), province × quarter cells with ≥100 tests; national figure is the test-weighted mean of those cells. Authors: Chissanupun Athiwarikanon, Kunanont Malayanont, Pakkapon Pattanakul, Sukumal Kitisin · Kasetsart University. Code and data on GitHub.
